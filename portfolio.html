01 / About

Researching machines that can understand, coordinate, and act.

I'm a mechatronics engineer and researcher working at the intersection of robotics, computer vision, and artificial intelligence. My work centers on multi-robot coordination, autonomous exploration, and human-robot interaction, with applications spanning disaster response, industrial automation, and healthcare robotics. I teach and mentor undergraduate and postgraduate students in robotics and AI, and collaborate with researchers across mechatronics and computer science on funded, publication-driven projects.
